Programmed Arduino Mega 2560 firmware with dual DS18B20 zones, dual heater relays, dual aeration, and I2C LCD status.
- **Supervision:** Prof. Dr. Salah Badr, Dr. Ragab Qasem
- **Interface:** Kivy/KivyMD monitoring with multi-threaded sensor streaming
- **Protocol:** HC-05 Bluetooth for remote process control
